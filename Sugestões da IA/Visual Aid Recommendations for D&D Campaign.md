## Map Creation

### World Map

- **Style Options**:
    - Hand-drawn fantasy style with mountains, forests, and settlements
    - Parchment-style aged map with decorative borders
    - Color-coded political map showing different territories
- **Key Elements to Include**:
    - Cities of Angá, Dustbrook, and Lahn with distinctive icons
    - Major geographical features (mountains, rivers, forests)
    - Travel routes connecting settlements
    - Borders between different regions or territories
    - Scale indicator and compass rose
- **Tools for Creation**:
    - Inkarnate (web-based fantasy map maker)
    - Wonderdraft (desktop software for fantasy maps)
    - GIMP or Photoshop for digital painting
    - Traditional media (watercolor, ink) for hand-drawn maps

### City Maps

#### Cidade de Angá

- **Style Recommendation**: Detailed overhead view with important buildings highlighted
- **Key Elements to Include**:
    - Temple district dedicated to Angá
    - Government buildings
    - Market areas
    - Distinct neighborhoods for different races
    - City walls and gates
    - Notable landmarks
- **Tools for Creation**:
    - Watabou Medieval Fantasy City Generator (for initial layout)
    - DungeonFog (for detailed city mapping)
    - Inkarnate Pro (city assets)

#### Dustbrook

- **Style Recommendation**: Rustic settlement map with surrounding terrain
- **Key Elements to Include**:
    - Main thoroughfare
    - Important buildings
    - Industry areas (mines, mills, etc.)
    - Surrounding farms or wilderness
- **Tools for Creation**:
    - Inkarnate
    - DungeonDraft
    - Hand-drawn sketches

#### Lahn

- **Style Recommendation**: Simple settlement map focusing on key locations
- **Key Elements to Include**:
    - Layout based on geographic setting (coastal, mountain, etc.)
    - Important buildings and landmarks
    - Defensive structures if applicable
- **Tools for Creation**:
    - Similar tools as above, scaled to settlement size

### Long-term Visual Development

- Create a visual style guide for consistent aesthetics
- Develop new visual aids as the campaign progresses
- Build a library of reusable assets
- Consider involving artistically inclined players in creation process
